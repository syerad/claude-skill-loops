# Loop Recipes

Show the person what loop shapes exist, fast. This skill only lists and
explains; the moment they pick one, hand off to the build skill.

## 1. Read the gallery

Read `../build/references/recipes.md` relative to this skill's
base directory — it is the single source of truth. Do not list recipes
from memory.

## 2. Present the catalog

Group by audience (the Roles tag), "anyone" recipes last. One line per
recipe: **name** · recurring or iterative · the Problem line, compressed
to one sentence. No iteration details, no tool requirements — this is a
menu, not the cookbook.

If the person has told you their role (or it is obvious from context),
put their role's group first and say so.

## 3. Hand off

End with one line: to adapt a recipe, say "build the <recipe name>
loop" — the build skill grounds its design in the recipe and tailors the
sources, cadence, and output in a short interview. Recipes are starting
points, not templates.

If they ask for a shape that is not in the gallery, say it is not there
and offer to design it from scratch with the build skill. If they have
built a loop their team copies, point at the contributing section of
the repo README.
